How to Read the Market Scanner

1. Evaluate the market

  • Check Status: CANDIDATE means no rejection reasons; WATCH means soft rejection reasons with score ≥ 60; REJECTED means hard rejection reasons or low score with rejections.
  • Use Market Score to compare overall suitability between pairs.
  • Check 24h Turnover to evaluate activity and liquidity.

2. Evaluate entry timing

  • Entry Score reflects the scanner entry-timing score (0–100).
  • Entry can be READY, WAIT_PULLBACK, WAIT_BREAKOUT_RETEST, WAIT_CONFIRMATION, or NO_ENTRY.
  • A high Market Score does not automatically mean immediate entry.
  • If the recommendation says to wait, do not treat score alone as a buy signal.

3. Confirm market conditions

  • 1H Trend shows the primary trend direction.
  • 15M Trend adds short-term trend confirmation.
  • Position shows where price sits in its calculated range (0% = range low, 100% = range high).
  • 1H Range shows the 1-hour range size as a percent move.

4. Important risk considerations

  • The scanner finds potential candidates; it does not guarantee profits.
  • High scores do not remove downside risk.
  • Support can break and breakouts can fail.
  • Before launching a Spot Grid Bot, review range, liquidity, fees, and out-of-range risk.
